# Workout Library

Every session has three parts - warm-up, main work, cool-down - and its `durationMinutes` covers
all three. Scale volume to the athlete; the structures stay the same.

**Standard warm-up (10-12 min)**: 5-6 min easy jog or erg → leg swings, world's greatest
stretch, air squats, inchworms → 3 × 20 s pick-ups. Add 1-2 light ramp sets before strength or
sled work. **Standard cool-down (5-8 min)**: easy jog/walk, then hips, calves, lats.

## Running

| Name                 | Type      | Main set                                        | Intensity           | Phase           |
| -------------------- | --------- | ----------------------------------------------- | ------------------- | --------------- |
| Easy run             | endurance | 30-60 min continuous                            | Zone 2, RPE 4       | All             |
| Long run             | long      | 60-110 min; last 15 min steady in build         | Zone 2 (→ Zone 3)   | All             |
| Strides              | technique | Easy run + 6 × 20 s fast / 60 s walk            | Relaxed fast        | Base            |
| Steady intervals     | tempo     | 6-8 × 2-3 min / 1 min jog                       | Zone 3, RPE 6       | Base            |
| Cruise intervals     | threshold | 4-6 × 5 min / 90 s jog, or 5-6 × 1 km / 75-90 s | T pace, RPE 7       | Base, Build     |
| Tempo run            | threshold | 20-30 min continuous                            | T + 5-10 s/km       | Build           |
| VO2 repeats          | vo2max    | 5-6 × 800 m / 2 min jog, or 10 × 400 m / 75 s   | I pace, RPE 8-9     | Build           |
| Hills                | hills     | 8-10 × 45-60 s uphill / jog down                | RPE 8               | Base, Build     |
| Race-pace kilometers | intervals | 8 × 1 km / 60-90 s walk-jog                     | Hyrox pace, RPE 6-7 | Specific        |
| Fartlek              | intervals | 8-10 × (1 min hard / 1 min easy)                | RPE 8 / 3           | Any, low stress |

Treadmill is fine (1% incline). Run-limited athletes: replace VO2 work with more easy volume.

## Erg (SkiErg and row)

| Name               | Main set                                                                          | Intensity          |
| ------------------ | --------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | ------------------ |
| Technique          | 6-8 × 250 m with 60 s rest, one cue each (ski: hinge + lats; row: legs-body-arms) | Zone 2             |
| Steady erg         | 3 × 2000 m alternating row / ski, 2 min rest                                      | Zone 2-3           |
| Threshold 500s     | 6-8 × 500 m / 60 s rest, alternate machines                                       | Zone 4             |
| Race-pace 1000s    | 3-4 × 1000 m / 2 min rest; hold the same split throughout                         | Zone 3 (race pace) |
| Ski-row-run triple | 4 rounds: 500 m ski + 500 m row + 800 m run, 2 min rest                           | Race effort        |

Cues: SkiErg at 40-45 strokes/min with a hip hinge, not all arms. Rower at 24-28 strokes/min,
damper 4-6; strong legs, long strokes. Both stations should feel like controlled breathing.

## Strength

**Max / general strength (base)** - 4 × 4-6, 2-3 min rest, RPE 7-8, leave 2 reps in reserve.

| Day A (squat / push)             | Day B (hinge / pull)                             |
| -------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------ |
| Back or front squat              | Trap-bar or conventional deadlift                |
| Bulgarian split squat 3 × 8/side | Hip thrust or RDL 3 × 8                          |
| Overhead press 4 × 6             | Pull-ups or lat pulldown 4 × 6-8                 |
| Push-ups / dips 3 × 10           | Bent-over or seated row 4 × 8                    |
| Core: plank 3 × 45 s, dead bug   | Farmers carry 4 × 40 m heavy; hanging knee raise |

**Strength endurance (build)** - 3 × 12-20, 60-90 s rest, RPE 7-8. This is the quality ESN's
plan puts next to endurance as the goal of Hyrox preparation.

- Goblet or front squat 3 × 15 → thrusters 3 × 15 (wall-ball strength)
- Walking lunges with sandbag/dumbbells 3 × 20 m → up to 4 × 25 m at race weight
- Sled push 6 × 12.5 m at race weight (+10-20% once comfortable), 60 s rest
- Sled pull 4 × 12.5 m, hand-over-hand, hips low
- Seated cable row or ring row 3 × 15; triceps push-down or dips 3 × 15 (SkiErg)
- Farmers carry 4 × 50 m at race weight, then heavier for 25 m
- Core: suitcase carry, pallof press, back extension

**Maintenance (specific, taper)** - 2 × 5-8 on squat, deadlift, press, pull. Fast, no grinding.

## Stations: technique, pacing and how to train them

| Station            | Technique cues                                                        | Race pacing                                                   | Training sets                                       | Substitute if no equipment                                            |
| ------------------ | --------------------------------------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------- | --------------------------------------------------- | --------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| SkiErg             | Hinge at hips, arms long, finish past thighs, breathe out on the pull | Zone 3, even split; do not chase the first 200 m              | 4 × 500 m, 3 × 1000 m                               | Banded straight-arm pulldowns, slam balls                             |
| Sled Push          | Arms straight or forearms on poles, hips low, short fast steps        | 12.5 m continuous, 10-15 s breath at each line                | 6-8 × 12.5 m race weight; heavy 4 × 10 m            | Treadmill push (belt off), plate push, heavy prowler on any surface   |
| Sled Pull          | Sit back, hips low, long pulls, walk backwards when arms fatigue      | Steady hand-over-hand; rope management matters                | 4-6 × 12.5 m; seated rope pulls                     | Heavy cable/band rows, rope pull on rower handle, backwards sled drag |
| Burpee Broad Jumps | Step down / step up to save HR, jump only as far as is repeatable     | Constant rhythm, ~1.6-2 m jumps, no sprinting                 | 4 × 20 m, EMOM 6-8 reps, 80 m for time (late build) | Same movement anywhere                                                |
| Row                | Legs drive first, relaxed grip, 24-28 spm                             | Zone 3; use it to bring HR down before the carry              | 3 × 1000 m, 500 m repeats                           | SkiErg, bike erg                                                      |
| Farmers Carry      | Tall posture, shoulders packed, quick small steps                     | Unbroken if possible; if not, one planned 5 s drop            | 4 × 50 m race weight; 2 × 100 m; heavy 4 × 25 m     | Dumbbells, trap bar                                                   |
| Sandbag Lunges     | Bag high on shoulders, knee kisses floor, drive through front heel    | Never stop with the bag up; steady 1 step per second          | 4 × 25 m; 2 × 50 m; 100 m after a 1 km run          | Dumbbell or barbell walking lunges                                    |
| Wall Balls         | Catch high, ride the ball down, breathe out on the throw, elbows in   | Planned sets from rep 1 (e.g. 25-20-20-15-10-10), 5-8 s rests | 5 × 20, 4 × 25, 100 for time on tired legs          | Thrusters with dumbbells or a light bar                               |

## Hyrox-specific sessions

**Compromised running** (`sport: hyrox`, `type: compromised`) - the signature session. Run at
Hyrox pace, go straight into a station, straight back out. No rest inside a round.

| Name                | Structure                                                                            | Phase     |
| ------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------ | --------- |
| Intro bricks        | 4 × (600 m run + 1 light station), 2 min rest                                        | Base late |
| Leg-killer runs     | 4-5 × (1 km run + sled push 25 m **or** 20 m lunges **or** 25 wall balls), 90 s rest | Build     |
| Upper/lower split   | 3 rounds: 1 km + 500 m ski + 1 km + 25 m sled push, 3 min rest                       | Build     |
| Roxzone repeats     | 8 × (400 m run + 60-90 s station at race pace), jog transitions, no rest             | Specific  |
| Back-half rehearsal | Row 1000 m + 1 km + farmers 200 m + 1 km + lunges 100 m + 1 km + 100 wall balls      | Specific  |

**Station circuits** (`sport: stations`) - station fitness without the running load.

| Name                      | Structure                                                                                                               |
| ------------------------- | ----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Technique circuit         | 3 rounds, full rest: 250 m ski, 12.5 m push, 12.5 m pull, 10 m BBJ, 250 m row, 50 m carry, 12.5 m lunges, 15 wall balls |
| EMOM 32                   | Every minute for 32 min, rotate: 12 cal ski / 10 m BBJ / 15 wall balls / 40 m carry                                     |
| Strength-endurance ladder | 5 rounds: 25 m sled push, 25 m sled pull, 20 wall balls, 20 m lunges; 2 min rest                                        |
| Grip + legs               | 4 rounds: 100 m farmers carry, 25 m lunges, 200 m row easy                                                              |

**Simulations** (`sport: hyrox`, `type: simulation`)

| Name            | Structure                                                                          | When                             |
| --------------- | ---------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | -------------------------------- |
| Half simulation | Stations 1-4 or 5-8 with the 1 km runs, race effort                                | Late build; alternate halves     |
| 3/4 simulation  | 6 runs + 6 stations, or all 8 at 50-75% distance                                   | Early specific                   |
| Full simulation | All 8 × (1 km + station) at race weight, 90-95% effort, practise fuel              | Once, 14-21 days out. Twice max. |
| Hyrox PFT       | 1 km run, 50 BBJ, 100 lunges, 1000 m row, 30 hand-release push-ups, 100 wall balls | Benchmark, base and mid-plan     |

Log station splits in every simulation and write them into the next plan revision.

## Recovery and mobility

- Recovery session: 20-30 min Zone 1 bike, walk or easy erg + 15 min mobility.
- Priority areas for Hyrox: ankles and calves (running, sleds), hips and quads (lunges, wall
  balls), thoracic spine and lats (SkiErg, wall balls), forearms (carry, sled pull).
- Cross-training (`sport: bike`) replaces easy runs 1:1.3 by time for athletes with impact
  injuries or high body weight.

## Writing `humanReadable`

Use the same three-part layout every time, with times that add up to `durationMinutes`:

```
Warm-up: 10 min easy jog, mobility, 3 x 20 s pick-ups
Main (40 min): 4 rounds, 90 s rest
  1 km run @ 5:10/km
  Sled push 25 m @ 152 kg
Cool-down: 10 min walk + stretch
```

Always state loads in the athlete's division weights and paces from their zones - never
"moderate weight" or "race pace" without the number.
