# The Hyrox Race

Every Hyrox race is identical worldwide: 8 × (1 km run + 1 station), always in this order.
Between the running track and the stations sits the **Roxzone** (transition area); time spent
there counts and typically adds 4-8 minutes plus 500-900 m of extra running over the race.

| #   | Station            | Distance / reps | What it taxes                            |
| --- | ------------------ | --------------- | ---------------------------------------- |
| 1   | SkiErg             | 1000 m          | Lats, triceps, core; first HR spike      |
| 2   | Sled Push          | 50 m (4 × 12.5) | Quads, glutes, calves; biggest leg drain |
| 3   | Sled Pull          | 50 m (4 × 12.5) | Posterior chain, grip, biceps            |
| 4   | Burpee Broad Jumps | 80 m            | Whole body; highest HR for most athletes |
| 5   | Row                | 1000 m          | Legs + back; partial recovery if paced   |
| 6   | Farmers Carry      | 200 m           | Grip, traps, core                        |
| 7   | Sandbag Lunges     | 100 m           | Glutes, quads; ruins run 8 if rushed     |
| 8   | Wall Balls         | 100 reps        | Quads, shoulders; finish on empty legs   |

## Divisions and weights

Weights below follow the official rulebook used in recent seasons. Hyrox adjusts details between
seasons (wall ball reps and targets, sled weights on different carpet), so **confirm against the
current rulebook at hyrox.com/rulebook before finalizing race-pace loads** and tell the athlete
you did or could not.

| Station (load)             | Women Open   | Women Pro    | Men Open     | Men Pro      |
| -------------------------- | ------------ | ------------ | ------------ | ------------ |
| Sled Push (incl. sled)     | 102 kg       | 152 kg       | 152 kg       | 202 kg       |
| Sled Pull (incl. sled)     | 78 kg        | 103 kg       | 103 kg       | 153 kg       |
| Farmers Carry (per hand)   | 16 kg        | 24 kg        | 24 kg        | 32 kg        |
| Sandbag Lunges             | 10 kg        | 20 kg        | 20 kg        | 30 kg        |
| Wall Balls (ball / target) | 4 kg / 2.7 m | 6 kg / 2.7 m | 6 kg / 3.0 m | 9 kg / 3.0 m |

- **Doubles** (women, men, mixed): both run every kilometer together; station work is split
  freely. Weights follow the Open division of the respective gender (mixed doubles use men's
  Open weights for sleds/carry/lunges in most seasons - verify).
- **Relay**: four athletes, each does 2 × (1 km + station). Open weights.
- Burpee broad jumps, SkiErg and row are identical in all divisions.

## Finish-time landscape

Average finish time across all divisions is roughly 90 minutes. Use this to place the athlete:

| Level        | Men Open    | Women Open  | Typical profile                               |
| ------------ | ----------- | ----------- | --------------------------------------------- |
| First-timer  | 1:35 - 1:55 | 1:45 - 2:05 | Runs 10 km in 60-70 min, new to stations      |
| Intermediate | 1:20 - 1:35 | 1:30 - 1:45 | 10 km in 50-58 min, trains 4-5×/week          |
| Advanced     | 1:10 - 1:20 | 1:18 - 1:30 | 10 km in 42-48 min, strong strength endurance |
| Competitive  | < 1:10      | < 1:18      | 10 km < 42 min, Pro division candidates       |

## Where the time goes

Running is about half of total race time, and the "compromised" kilometers after sleds, burpees
and lunges are where races are won or lost. For a 1:30 finisher a typical split is:

| Segment               | Time    | Share |
| --------------------- | ------- | ----- |
| 8 × 1 km runs         | ~44 min | 49%   |
| 8 stations            | ~39 min | 43%   |
| Roxzone (transitions) | ~7 min  | 8%    |

Typical station times (intermediate, Open): SkiErg 4:30, Sled Push 3:15, Sled Pull 5:00,
Burpee Broad Jumps 5:30, Row 4:45, Farmers Carry 2:15, Sandbag Lunges 5:00, Wall Balls 6:30.

## Movement standards worth coaching early

Penalties and no-reps cost more than fitness gaps for first-timers. Hyrox itself puts learning
the movement standards at the top of its preparation advice.

- **Burpee broad jump**: chest touches the floor, hands are placed no more than one foot-length
  ahead of the feet, and both feet take off and land together. Stepping back down and stepping
  up out of the burpee is allowed; extra steps between jump and burpee are not.
- **Sandbag lunges**: trailing knee touches the floor every rep, full hip extension at the top,
  bag stays on the shoulders; no resting with the bag on the floor.
- **Wall balls**: hip crease below knee at the bottom, ball hits the target (centre plate).
- **Sled pull**: stay inside the athlete box, do not step on or over the front line.
- **Sled push**: the whole sled must cross the line at each 12.5 m.
- **Farmers carry**: kettlebells may be set down, but not dropped.
